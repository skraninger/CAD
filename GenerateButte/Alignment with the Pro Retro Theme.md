**Alignment with the "Pro Retro" Theme**

Your Sedona mesa concept aligns partially, but may miss the core emotional intent of the "Pro Retro" theme if executed only as a static landscape shape.

According to the [contest details](https://fluoraplant.com/pages/pro-retro-lamp-shade-contest), **Pro Retro** focuses on "lighting as vessels for memory and inheritance" and objects that visually record history over time (like tree rings, sedimentary rock layers, or family quilts).

- **Where it works:** Sedimentary rock layers naturally represent environmental time and history, which directly matches the contest’s mention of geological layers as inspiration.
- **Where it falls short:** The theme emphasizes creating a object that accumulates *personal human history* or provenance across generations to avoid obsolescence, rather than just replicating a vintage or natural aesthetic.

------

**Ideas to Strengthen or Pivot the Concept**

- **Layered Physical Growth (Sedona Variant):** Instead of a pre-formed mesa, design a modular mesa where distinct "strata" rings can be added, swapped, or custom-engraved by the owner over milestones in their life (e.g., year, location, personal notes). The Monos light then illuminates through these personalized translucent rock layers.
- **The "Mid-Century Modern" Memory Shade:** Create a geometric, stacked mid-century silhouette where panels or slatted layers can be rearranged, altered, or marked by successive owners, leaning into recognizable retro industrial design while preserving the "vessel of memory" prompt.
- **Interactive Stratigraphy:** Use 3D-printed translucent materials (like PETG or PLA) with varying wall thicknesses so that gesture controls or dynamic light animations on the Monos light reveal hidden patterns or text embedded within the walls—acting like historical "fossils" appearing under different light modes.

------

**Best Process to Create the 3D Design for Printing**

1. **Get the Monos Dimensions & Assets:** Start by downloading the official 3D CAD files and assets from the [Fluora Contest Page](https://fluoraplant.com/pages/pro-retro-lamp-shade-contest) to ensure accurate clearance and mounting fit around the Monos core (61mm tapering to 55.5mm square base, 240mm height).
2. **CAD / 3D Modeling Software Options:**
   - **Blender (Free):** Ideal if you are sculpting organic, natural rock textures, erosion lines, and terrain-like meshes.
   - **Fusion 360 or Onshape (Free for personal/hobby use):** Ideal for precise mechanical attachment, modular interlocking parts, or geometric mid-century shapes.
   - **S3DF / TopoMap generators:** You can convert real-world elevation data (DEM files of Sedona) into 3D printable STL files using tools like *TouchTerrain* or *MapToGlobe*, then import the geometry into your CAD software to hollow out the center for the light engine.
3. **Optimizing for 3D Printing & Lighting:**
   - **Lithophane / Thickness Effects:** Vary wall thickness between 0.8mm and 3.0mm. Thinner sections will allow the Monos's 256 addressable LEDs to shine brightly in vibrant Sedona reds and oranges, while thicker sections cast deep shadows.
   - **Material Selection:** Use Translucent Natural PLA, PETG, or diffusion-focused filaments so the 3D animations bleed through smoothly without visible LED hot spots.
   - **Orientation & Supports:** Design vertical relief lines to minimize support material inside the cavity where the Monos slides in.
