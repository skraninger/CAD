## Printing Instructions

### Corners:

- Print at 45 degrees on the corner
- Brim to support 45 degree printet
- Perimeters, Top, Bottom - 3
- Infill - 100% Rectilinear, Top Monotonic Lines, Bottom Monotonic
- Support Material - Snug, Concentric interface pattern

### 90 Degree Clamp:

- Print at 45 degrees along longer edge
- Manual supports on either side to support 45 degree print
- Perimeters, Top, Bottom - 5
- Infill - 30% gyroid, Top and Bottom Archimedean Chords
- Support Material - Organic

### T Clamp:

- Print on clamp face with flagpole element supported
- Rectangular manual support 88x40x7 centered under flagpole tube
- Perimeters, Top, Bottom - 4
- Infill - 25% gyroid, Top and Bottom Concentric
- Support Material - Snug, Concentric interface pattern
